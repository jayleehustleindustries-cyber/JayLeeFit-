## 2. Content mechanics that actually blow up

From short-form/reselling viral research — **the transferable part is mechanics,
not the product niche.** (You resell a coaching *system*, not thrifted clothes;
copy how they film, not what they sell.)

### 🚀 Will blow up (do these)
- **3-second hook, no runway.** 63% of top posts get to the point instantly.
  Frame 1 must state the payoff.
- **Reverse-psychology hooks:** "Don't start coaching with me if…", "Not for
  everyone." Curiosity > hype.
- **Grit→reward story arc:** gym struggle → rooftop payoff. Your video already
  has both halves — cut them hook-first.
- **Real text overlay + captions** (Descript), not baked-in AI text.
- **Repeat winning *formats*, not hashtags.** Save 10–15 winners, tag by angle
  (before/after, myth-bust, day-in-the-life), remake the format.
- **Same time weekly** — Wed–Fri 10:00 PT (your data), best asset first 60s.

### 🟡 Doable / worth testing
- Countdown/on-screen timers for retention.
- A/B two hooks on the same body; keep the winner.
- Live sessions (Q&A, plan breakdowns) once cadence is steady.

### ⚪ Irrelevant / skip (the noise)
- Product-category reselling content itself (thrift hauls, sneaker flips) — wrong
  niche, doesn't transfer.
- Chasing trending *audio* with no tie to your offer.
- Follower-count vanity — track intent (clicks/DMs/intake), not likes.
- Cloning a specific competitor's footage — legally risky, and undifferentiated.

### Honest limits
- eBay/Poshmark don't expose "past viral analytics" to pull — no API for that.
  The real data you own is your **Metricool** heatmap + your own post results.
- Virality is probabilistic. The system's job is **more shots on target**
  (consistent, hook-first, QA'd), not a guaranteed hit.

## 3. How this plugs into the pipeline

- **Media Engine** generates in this grade + avatar look.
- **Editing/Clipping** (Descript) adds real hooks/captions, strips AI text.
- **QA** kills anything with baked AI text, watermarks, or a weak 3-sec hook.
- **Publish** drops Wed–Fri 10:00 PT.
- Winners logged back to Airtable → Analytics remakes the format next week.
