# JayLeeFit — Marketing SOP & Content Engine

A repeatable operating procedure for growing reach as an online fitness coach.
The goal is not "go viral" — it's a **system that runs every week** and feeds
the client funnel (social → intake → Airtable → bot → app → web).

---

## 1. The one rule

**Every asset points to one CTA: the intake link.** If a post, reel, or story
does not move someone one step closer to booking, it is brand-building only —
keep it to ~20% of output.

---

## 2. Marketing asset structure (the "asset stack")

Build assets in layers so one piece of work produces many posts.

| Layer | What it is | Made from | Reuse |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Pillar** | 1 long asset/week (client transformation breakdown, "how I program macros") | Real client data from Airtable (Progress Tracking + macro %) | Source of everything below |
| **Cut-downs** | 3–5 short reels/clips from the pillar | The pillar | Daily posting |
| **Proof** | Before/after, metric deltas, macro screenshots | Progress Tracking + Nutrition Plans | Highest conversion |
| **Authority** | Tips, myth-busting, Q&A | Your coaching POV | Trust/saves |
| **CTA** | "DM me / link in bio" intake push | n/a | 1–2x per week hard, soft daily |

**Branded template kit** (your "creation style"): one consistent look —
fixed fonts, color, intro/outro, caption format. Lock it once so every asset
is recognizably yours and fast to produce.

---

## 3. Weekly SOP (repeatable checklist)

**Monday — Plan (30 min)**
- [ ] Pick the week's pillar topic (pull a real client win from Airtable).
- [ ] Confirm the single CTA and intake link.

**Tuesday — Produce (90 min)**
- [ ] Record/build the pillar asset using the branded template.
- [ ] Generate 3–5 cut-downs.

**Wednesday–Sunday — Publish (15 min/day)**
- [ ] 1 post/day across chosen platforms, each with a CTA in caption.
- [ ] Stories daily: behind-the-scenes + 1 proof asset.
- [ ] Reply to every comment/DM within 24h (this is the conversion step).

**Sunday — Review (20 min)**
- [ ] Log the week's metrics (below). Double down on the top performer.

---

## 4. What to measure (keep it to 5)

Vanity metrics lie. Track the funnel:
1. **Reach / views** (awareness)
2. **Profile visits** (interest)
3. **Link clicks / DMs started** (intent)
4. **Intake form completions** (leads)
5. **New paying clients** (revenue)

> Conversion is the only number that pays. A small account with a tight funnel
> beats a big account with no CTA.

---

## 5. Positioning (instead of "targeting competitors")

Win the niche on a **visible system**, not call-outs:
- Show the *exact* loop most coaches hide — macro % math, weekly check-ins,
  metric deltas. Transparency is the moat.
- Pick one avatar (e.g. "busy professionals, fat-loss, 12-week") and speak only
  to them. Specific beats broad.
- Differentiate on **responsiveness** (24h reply SOP) and **proof**, which are
  operational advantages you control.

---

## 6. Where this connects to the build

- Proof assets pull straight from the **Airtable Client Hub** (Progress
  Tracking photos + macro %). The data layer is also the content engine.
- Scheduling/analytics can later run through a tool (e.g. Metricool) so posting
  becomes part of the SOP, not a daily scramble.

*Run the loop for 8 weeks before changing the system. Consistency is the
strategy.*
