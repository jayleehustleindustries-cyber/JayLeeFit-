## RULES — agents load this block first (non-negotiables from the brief)

1. **No fake income claims. No fake sold comps. No invented item values,
   platform analytics, or results.** Real numbers come from the EHC
   Inventory Log V2 / real sales (e.g. the Hoka Bondi 8 that sold on
   eBay 2026-07-09) or they don't get said.
2. **No fake app/product promises** until the product exists. The only
   CTAs allowed now: follow for the result, comment BUY/PASS, save
   this, waitlist/checklist once one actually exists.
3. **No copying creator scripts, assets, or identities.** Research
   extracts *patterns* from public content only.
4. **No publishing without human approval. No account login, payment, or
   final-submit action by any agent.**
5. **Every video teaches one useful decision** (buy/pass, list/hold,
   keep/sell, train/rest, focus/drop) **and points somewhere** (follow
   for result, comment vote, checklist, listing).
6. **Episode skeleton is mandatory:** pattern interrupt → claim →
   investigation → decision → meaning → CTA.
7. **Quality bar (YouTube policy):** original commentary, real evidence,
   or a distinct story arc in every video — zero mass-produced filler.
8. **Spend gates:** any paid generation (Higgsfield etc.) needs a cost
   preview + human go-ahead. Lost Things production additionally obeys
   `../lost-things-studio/RULES.md`.
9. **Daily lanes** (from the brief): morning = fitness lane (separate
   calendar), midday = research, afternoon = production, evening =
   review/queue.

## Visual system (all Object Story / Discipline / Signs episodes)

Dark inspection table or clean white sweep; cinematic close-ups of tags,
stitching, soles; receipt/proof overlays; subtle push-ins; strong
narration; NO face. Tone: mysterious, grounded, useful, never
fake-deep.

## Measurement & retro

Per post: completion %, rewatches, shares, follows (platform natives),
pulled at 24h/72h/1wk. Monthly gate: Lost Things continues only if Tests
1–2 show viewers finishing and returning FOR THE CHARACTERS; Hidden
Value Lab doubles the winning series and kills the weakest, decided in
the retro — proposals flagged for human review, never auto-launched.
