# EXAMPLES — copy this, not that

Read this before drafting. The gold standard is real (week 001, made by
hand with a frontier model). The failures are what cheaper models
actually produce when the SOP isn't followed.

## 1. Gold standard — week 001 (annotated)

| Day | Hook | Why it works (in rule terms) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 22 | "Your lat pulldown is a bicep curl with a cable bolted to the ceiling." | M1. Names exercise, states the wrong outcome (approved claim #1), 14 words, zero advice — the absurd image does the teaching. |
| Tue 23 | "I don't chase motivation. I bury it and build on top of it." | V1. First-person identity, contrast (chase vs. bury-and-build), never mentions the viewer. |
| Wed 24 | "Half-rep lateral raises grow your traps, not your delts." | M2. Approved claim #2, different body part than Monday, 10 words. |

| Fri 26 | "If you need motivation to train, this brand isn't for you." | F1. Explicit exclusion clause; exactly one Filter this week, on Friday. |
| Sat 27 | "Built in the dark. Worn in the light." | V3. 8 words, two clauses, dark/light contrast. Aesthetic, not advice. |

What to notice across all 7: no emojis, no numbers, no questions, no
"you should", every hook a statement, every hook one of the pattern
shapes. That's not a coincidence — the patterns in HOOK-STANDARD §3 were
reverse-engineered from this week.

## 2. Failure gallery — real cheap-model output shapes, and the rule that catches each

| Bad hook | What a naive model was trying to do | Rule that kills it |
|---|---|---|
| "Are you making this lat pulldown mistake? 🤔" | Engagement-bait question | U2 (no questions), U3 (emoji) |
| "STOP doing half reps! Here's how to fix your lateral raises 💪" | Advice/urgency | U3, U5, U6 ("stop doing this") |
| "90% of gym-goers train their traps instead of delts without knowing it" | Fake authority | U4 (no statistics) |
| "Consistency is key. Show up every day and crush your goals!" | Generic motivation | U6 (two banned phrases), no pattern, no contrast |
| "You have it in you. Today is YOUR day. Let's go!" | Motivational Jay | U3 (caps), U6 ("let's go"), Villain definition (Jay never addresses viewer potential) |
| "Leg press grows your calves, not your quads." | Pattern-shaped but false | Mechanical approved-claims list (not on it) |
| "If you skip leg day, if you love excuses, if you quit early — leave." | Over-stacked Filter | ≤15 words, F-pattern shape (one condition, one exclusion) |
| "Built in the dark. Worn in the light." (again, week 003) | Copying gold verbatim | U7 (no phrase reuse), pattern cooldown |

## 3. The judgment that used to be invisible (now written down)

These were the autopilot calls the old process made without saying so:

1. **Jay never coaches in a hook.** Teaching belongs to Marie; Jay's job
   is identity and standard. Mixing them flattens both personas.
2. **Marie mocks the rep, never the person.** "Your lat pulldown is a
   bicep curl" attacks the movement. "You're doing it wrong" attacks the
   viewer — never that.
3. **One Filter per week, always end-of-work-week.** Exclusivity is a
   spice; two Filters read as contempt.
4. **Saturday is short.** Weekend scroll is fast; the Sat hook is the
   closest thing to merch copy ("Built in the dark. Worn in the light."
   could be printed on a shirt — that's the bar V3 encodes).
5. **Sunday closes the loop.** The recap hook quietly implies a
   community ("the standard held") without ever saying "we're a
   community" — V4's shape does this automatically.
6. **Rhetoric beats data.** "Most folded by Tuesday" persuades; "73%
   quit" invites fact-checking and violates the claims standard. This is
   why U4 is absolute.
