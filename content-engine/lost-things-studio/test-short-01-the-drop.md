# Test Short 01 — "The Drop" (30–45s)

**Purpose:** first public proof that the characters read, the world
reads, and the hook holds. Vertical-first (9:16), cut from 16:9 masters.
**Platforms:** YouTube Shorts, TikTok, IG Reels, FB Reels.
**Target runtime:** 38 seconds.
**Success gate:** completion rate + rewatches + follows, compared against
Test Short 02 before any pilot production.

## Logline

A mysterious battery falls into the under-couch world; a retired TV
remote decides it's destiny.

## Time-coded script

**0:00–0:02 — COLD OPEN, THE PUNCH**
VISUAL: Darkness. A metallic cylinder PUNCHES through the cushion seam
directly toward camera, backlit by a warm shaft of light. Dust ignites
in the beam.
SFX: THUNK. Fabric tear. Low room tone.
(No dialogue. Problem on screen inside 2 seconds: something big just
fell in.)

**0:02–0:08 — THE ROLL**
VISUAL: The battery bounces off a toy block — TING — and rolls through
a tiny city built of lost things (dice tower, comb fence, domino path),
knocking each one over in sequence. Wide shot establishes the world in
one move.
SFX: TING. RATTLE. Tiny crashes escalating in pitch.
NARRATOR (The House, dry, warm): "In the world under the couch, most
things arrived by accident."

**0:08–0:14 — THE STOP**
VISUAL: The battery rolls to a dead stop inches from CLICKER. Hold the
silence one full beat. His red power button reflects in the metal.
Slow push-in on his face: shock → awe.
CLICKER (whisper): "Not this."
CLICKER (suddenly loud, grand): "This is *destiny.*"

**0:14–0:22 — THE CLAIM**
VISUAL: Clicker throws himself bodily over the battery like it's a
treasure chest. Button marches in with a thread-and-label barricade.
BUTTON: "Unregistered object. Possible leakage. Nobody touch it."
VISUAL: Crumb's tiny foot enters frame and touches it immediately.
CRUMB: "It tastes like pennies."
PATCH: "Why do you know what pennies taste like?"
CRUMB: "Research."

**0:22–0:30 — THE WOUND**
VISUAL: Clicker pulls the battery close, defensive. Cut to his cracked
corner as he says the line — the pride and the damage in one frame.
CLICKER: "For your information, I was *designed* to accept two of
these."
BUTTON (flat): "You currently accept dust."
VISUAL: Clicker's glare. Crumb stifles a laugh and crumbles slightly.

**0:30–0:38 — THE HOOK OUT**
VISUAL: The battery begins to GLOW faintly. Everyone leans in. Cut to
black on the glow reflected in four pairs of eyes.
NARRATOR: "Nobody under the couch knew what it powered. They were about
to find out."
END CARD (2s max): LOST THINGS STUDIO mark + "Follow for what it
powers."
SFX: Rising hum, cut to silence.

## Character-emotion map

- Clicker: pride → hope → wounded dignity (the arc IS the short)
- Button: procedural alarm (comic constant)
- Crumb: pure curiosity (taste-tests reality)
- Patch: audience surrogate, deadpan questions

## Voice direction (per RULES.md #4)

- Narrator: warm, dry, grounded; 0.95x pace; emotion from restraint.
- Clicker: precise, proud, mildly sarcastic; vulnerability in pauses.
- Button: fast operational cadence, controlled anxiety, no shrieking.
- Crumb: bright, impulsive, sincere; never baby-talk.
- Patch: earnest, quick, one deadpan line — don't oversell it.
- Generate each line separately; normalize loudness; music under -18dB
  relative to dialogue.
