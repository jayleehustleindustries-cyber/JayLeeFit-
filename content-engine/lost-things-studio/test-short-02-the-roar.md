# Test Short 02 — "The Roar" (60–90s)

**Purpose:** second proof test — can the world sustain action-comedy and
a rescue beat? Tests retention on a longer arc + the vacuum as the
series monster.
**Target runtime:** 75 seconds.
**Depends on:** Test Short 01 approved cast sheet + environment locks.
Reuses its approved poses/environments wherever possible (RULES.md #10).

## Logline

The vacuum wakes. Crumb rides the battery straight toward the open edge
of the couch, and the crew's rescue only works if Clicker gives up the
thing he just claimed.

## Time-coded script

**0:00–0:02 — COLD OPEN**
VISUAL: A vacuum switch CLICKS in extreme close-up. The whole frame
vibrates. Dust lifts.
SFX: Click → sub-bass rumble.
(Problem on screen in 2 seconds.)

**0:02–0:10 — ROAR PROTOCOL**
VISUAL: Under-couch city shaking. Button slams a tiny alarm (a bell from
a cat collar).
BUTTON (horrified): "Roar protocol."
VISUAL: Low-angle: the vacuum head slides into view at the couch edge
like a ship's prow. Fear expressions, readable, not horror.
NARRATOR: "Every kingdom has a monster. Theirs had three settings and a
washable filter."

**0:10–0:22 — THE RUNAWAY**
VISUAL: Crumb, mid-joyride, is on the battery as it rolls downhill
through a fabric-fiber tunnel, accelerating toward the exposed edge —
and the vacuum's path.
BUTTON: "It is not a boat!"
CRUMB (accelerating, delighted then worried): "It is *becoming* a
boat!"
VISUAL COMEDY: Clicker tries to run, tips forward, slides like a sled.
Patch swings from a loose thread, misses, spins twice, crashes into
Clicker.

**0:22–0:34 — THE GAP**
VISUAL: The battery wedges across a split in the floorboards. Crumb
hangs from the far end. Suction visibly pulls dust past him in streaks.
PATCH (jumping onto the near end, counterweight): "Crumb, crawl toward
me!"
CRUMB: "I am mostly corners! Crawling is complicated!"
VISUAL: The battery starts sliding. Button does frantic thread-math,
then looks — at Clicker.
BUTTON: "Clicker. We need your whole weight."

**0:34–0:46 — THE CHOICE (the heart of the short)**
VISUAL: Close on Clicker. Above, muffled through the cushions:
HUMAN CHILD (muffled): "I like the old clicky one."
VISUAL: His cracked corner trembles. One beat. He could take the battery
and run. He throws himself onto the bridge.
CLICKER: "For the record, this is not my whole weight. I am carrying
several unresolved updates."
VISUAL: Bridge stabilizes. Patch hauls Crumb up.

**0:46–0:60 — POWER**
VISUAL: The vacuum head turns toward them. Button points at an
abandoned toy car.
BUTTON: "Battery. Car. Now."
VISUAL: Clicker snaps the battery in with one practiced motion —
headlights ON. Everyone piles in; Clicker takes the wheel.
CLICKER: "Finally. A device with physical controls."
VISUAL: The car tears away as the vacuum devours the tunnel behind them
— debris, dust, near-misses. Biggest visual beat of the short.

**0:60–0:70 — AFTER THE ROAR**
VISUAL: Quiet. The vacuum shuts off mid-frame; dust settles like snow.
CRUMB: "You gave up your battery."
CLICKER: "Temporary reassignment."
PATCH: "You saved us."
CLICKER: "Naturally. I am the remote."
VISUAL: His red power button glows faintly from residual charge.
Everyone notices. Nobody says anything. Warmest frame of the short.

**0:70–0:75 — TAG + OUT**
VISUAL: A SECOND battery falls from above and lands directly on Button.
BUTTON (muffled): "Unregistered object."
CRUMB (licks it): "Also pennies."
END CARD: LOST THINGS STUDIO mark + "Follow — the couch isn't done."
